Nova Arcade

Play Here: Nova Arcade

Nova Arcade is a collection of 22 browser games in one arcade, spanning action, strategy and adventure. It includes titles like Sketch & Slay, Cave to Empire, Tiny Town and Creature Quest.

The arcade keeps track of your plays and best scores, lets you favorite games and jump back into ones you've played, and has a "Surprise me" button if you can't decide.
